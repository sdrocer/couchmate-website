Couchmate Helper for Mac

The free companion for Couchmate on your iPhone or iPad.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

You're not on a Mac. Open this page on your Mac: getcouchmate.app/mac

  1. Open the download Open CouchmateHelper.dmg from your Downloads. A window with the helper and your Applications folder appears.
  2. Drag it into Applications Then open Couchmate Helper from Applications. Your Mac asks once whether to open an app from the internet: click Open.
  3. Allow Accessibility The helper's window shows you how. It lets Couchmate press play, move the pointer and type for you. It never sees your screen.

Back on your iPhone or iPad, Couchmate finds your Mac by itself.

Using Chrome? If it holds the download back, open its downloads list and choose Keep. Safari downloads it directly.

Couchmate collects no data. The helper only talks to your own devices on your Wi-Fi.
