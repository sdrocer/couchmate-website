Made for movies and shows.

  • Skip Intro with one tapWhen a streaming site offers to skip the intro or the recap, Couchmate shows it as one big button.
  • On to the next episode⏭ starts the next episode on sites that offer one.
  • Quick launchOpen your streaming services on the Mac with one tap.

Everything from the sofa.

  • The buttons you reach forPlay, pause, skip and volume, also from the Lock Screen and with Siri.
  • A trackpad in your handMove the pointer, click and scroll. One hand is enough: slide along the edge to scroll.
  • A keyboard when you need oneIt opens by itself when you tap a search field on the Mac.

Falls asleep when you do.

  • Sleep timerThe sound fades out and the film pauses after 15 minutes up to 2 hours.
  • The Mac sleeps tooIf you like, the Mac goes to sleep right after, so it doesn't run all night.

Your iPad, too.

  • The iPad as a remoteThe same remote, on the bigger screen.
  • Or a remote for the iPadYour iPhone becomes a Bluetooth remote for your iPad: play, pause, volume and a pointer.

Private by design.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Couchmate talks only to your own devices on your Wi-Fi, encrypted, and never sees your screen.

Free to download, with every feature free for 14 days. After that, play/pause and volume stay free, and a one-time unlock adds the rest. No subscription.
